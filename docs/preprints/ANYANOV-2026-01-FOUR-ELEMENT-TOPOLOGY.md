## 1. Introduction: The Crisis of Open-Loop Mental Models

Such conceptualizations violate the foundational thermodynamic constraint governing all natural biological structures: **the conservation of energy in closed dissipative systems** (Prigogine, 1977). ## 2. Mathematical Formalism and Circuit Architecture

The human conscious operator is formally defined as a single-loop closed circuit governed by Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL):

$$\sum_{k} V_k = \mathcal{E} - V_{\text{fuse}} - V_{\text{ego}} - V_{\text{load}} = 0$$

Under nominal operating regimes (where the Fuse impedance $Z_{\text{fuse}} \approx 0$), the total circuit impedance is the series sum of the parasitic internal resistance and the useful active load:

$$R_{\text{total}} = R_{\text{ego}} + R_{\text{load}}$$

The circulating current $I(t)$ produced by the somatic electromotive force $\mathcal{E}$ is determined by Ohm's Law:

$$I(t) = \frac{\mathcal{E}}{R_{\text{ego}}(t) + R_{\text{load}}}$$

### 2.1 The Four Elemental Nodes

1. **The Generator ($\mathcal{E}$, *Tanden* / Lower Dantian):**

   - **Crucial System Property:** $R_{\text{ego}}$ is the *only dynamically controllable variable* accessible to conscious metacognitive regulation.

   - Follows the principle of Emptiness (*Śūnyatā*): the load possesses zero inherent energy of its own; its luminescence is strictly a function of current flowing through it.

### 4.1 The Well-Being Index ($\mathcal{H}$)

The efficiency coefficient of consciousness ($\mathcal{H}$, the Happiness / Superconductivity Metric) is defined as the ratio of useful power dissipated across the load to the total energy expended by the somatic generator:

$$\mathcal{H} = \frac{P_{\text{load}}}{P_{\text{total}}} = \frac{I^2 R_{\text{load}}}{I^2 (R_{\text{load}} + R_{\text{ego}})} = \frac{R_{\text{load}}}{R_{\text{load}} + R_{\text{ego}}} = \frac{1}{1 + \frac{R_{\text{ego}}}{R_{\text{load}}}}$$

From this relation emerge two asymptotic states:

$$\lim_{R_{\text{ego}} \to 0} \mathcal{H} = 1 \quad (100\% \text{ Efficiency: State of Superconductivity / Mushin})$$

$$\lim_{R_{\text{ego}} \to \infty} \mathcal{H} = 0 \quad (0\% \text{ Efficiency: Total Ohmic Blockade / Neurosis})$$

### 4.2 The Joule–Lenz Law of Mental Suffering

By the Joule–Lenz Law, the thermal energy $Q$ dissipated across the parasitic resistor during time interval $\Delta t$ is:

$$Q_{\text{parasitic}} = \int_{0}^{\Delta t} I(t)^2 R_{\text{ego}}(t) \, dt$$

1. **Ohmic Overheating:** Thermal dissipation $Q_{\text{parasitic}}$ escalates quadratically relative to intention current $I$. Subjectively, this registers as emotional inflammation, agitation, neck/shoulder tension, and cognitive friction.
2. **Load Starvation:** The voltage drop across the load falls to near zero ($V_{\text{load}} = I R_{\text{load}} \to 0$). The individual experiences motivational paralysis ("choking under pressure").
3. **Protective Fuse Tripping (Burnout):** To prevent irreversible neurological degradation, the *Zanshin* circuit breaker trips, setting $I(t) \to 0$. Clinical depression, chronic apathy, and burnout are thus recognized not as organic failures, but as **protective system shutdowns** triggered by unmitigated parasitic overheating.
